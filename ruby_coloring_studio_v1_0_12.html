✏️ What Should Miss Ruby Draw?
Tell Miss Ruby what you want — up to 5 pages!
  • You can ask for different things on each page, or the same thing on all pages.
  • Example: "2 pages of puppies playing, 2 pages of dinosaurs, and 1 page of a mermaid underwater"
  • Example: "4 pages of unicorns in a magical forest"
  • Example: "Page 1 a cat, page 2 a dog, page 3 a bunny"

🚫 Character Names Won't Work!
Miss Ruby can't draw named characters like Spider-Man, Elsa, Mickey Mouse, or Pikachu — they're protected by copyright. Instead, describe what they look like! Try "a hero in a red and blue costume" or "a snow princess with a long braid" and Miss Ruby will make something just as cool! 🎨
